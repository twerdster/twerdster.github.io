About

I am CTO of an early-stage company. Previously, I was Incubation and ML Lead at Apple and CTO of Camerai, which was acquired. My academic work spans computer vision, machine learning, gesture recognition, and numerical geometry. I completed a direct-track PhD in Electrical Engineering at the Technion - Israel Institute of Technology, where I worked with Prof. Ron Kimmel in the Geometric Image Processing Lab. I received a BSc in Mechatronics from the University of Cape Town.

A randomized decision-tree learning framework designed for large datasets and parallel training on multiple GPUs. Tea was developed as part of my PhD research at the Technion.
