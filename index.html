About Me

I am CTO of an early-stage company building toward a future enabled by Physical AI. Previously, I led a technology incubation team at Apple, where we worked on some of the company's most ambitious projects, from real-time networks running on phones to autonomous robotics and the Vision Pro perception stack for different user experiences. Prior to joining Apple, I was CTO of Camerai, where we built AI training and inference systems for real-time video processing on phones.

My academic work spans computer vision, machine learning, gesture recognition, and numerical geometry. I received my PhD in Electrical Engineering from the Technion - Israel Institute of Technology, where I worked with Prof. Ron Kimmel in the Geometric Image Processing Lab. I received a BSc in Mechatronics from the University of Cape Town.
