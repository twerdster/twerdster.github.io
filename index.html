About

My academic work spans computer vision, machine learning, gesture recognition, and numerical geometry. I completed a direct-track PhD in Electrical Engineering at the Technion - Israel Institute of Technology, where I worked with Prof. Ron Kimmel in the Geometric Image Processing Lab. I received a BSc in Mechatronics from the University of Cape Town.

Code & Data

Tea

A randomized decision-tree learning framework designed for large datasets and parallel training on multiple GPUs. Tea was developed as part of my PhD research at the Technion.

code

HandNet

A depth-image dataset for hand and fingertip detection, collected with synchronized tracking and depth cameras. It accompanies the BMVC 2015 paper Rule of Thumb.
